## 1. How the Arabic works now

**No Arabic is authored anywhere in this repository.** `content/duas.ts` names
āyāt and, where a supplication sits inside a longer verse, a half-open word
range. `npm run content:fetch` resolves those against the `quran-uthmani`
edition and writes `content/generated/arabic.json` plus a SHA-256 per entry in
`content.lock.json`.

This is stricter than fetching once and committing, and the Phase 1 audit is
why. Both scripture errors it found — `sulaiman-27-19` carrying the text of
46:15, and `ayyub-38-41` with words inserted at an excerpt boundary — read
fluently, cited plausibly, and had translations that agreed with the corrupted
text. Review does not catch that. A hash does.

Verified working:

```
$ npx tsx scripts/verify-content.ts        # clean
✓ all 52 entries match content.lock.json   exit 0

$ # after flipping one character of one hash
✗ 1 problem(s):
  MISMATCH yunus-la-ilaha-illa-anta (21:87)
           locked  b3c8f086c2f18876…
           derived 83c8f086c2f18876…
                                           exit 1
```

Both Phase 1 errors are now correct at the source:

| Entry | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Sulaymān 27:19 | ended `وَأَصْلِحْ لِي فِي ذُرِّيَّتِي…` (46:15) | ends `وَأَدْخِلْنِى بِرَحْمَتِكَ فِى عِبَادِكَ ٱلصَّٰلِحِينَ` |
| Ayyūb 38:41 | `رَبِّ إِنِّي مَسَّنِيَ…` (words inserted) | `أَنِّى مَسَّنِىَ ٱلشَّيْطَٰنُ بِنُصْبٍۢ وَعَذَابٍ` |

### Excerpts are now data

44 of 52 entries are excerpts. Each records `fromWord`, `toWord`, and a note
saying what was cut, and the UI will disclose it. Every boundary was chosen by
reading `scripts/inspect-ayah.ts` output against a Pickthall gloss — never by
typing or recalling Arabic. The old corpus had 16 silent excerpts and no field
in which to record that fact.

Two apparatus characters are stripped before indexing, and only when they stand
alone as their own token: `۞` (rub-el-ḥizb) and the waqf marks `ۖ ۗ ۘ ۙ ۚ ۛ ۜ`.
They are scribal marks for reciters, not part of the āyah, and leaving them in
would shift excerpt indices depending on where a pause happens to fall.
Combining marks attached to letters are never touched.

## 2. Turkish

All 47 legacy Turkish translations are preserved in
`content/generated/legacy-migration.json`, lifted verbatim by
`scripts/migrate-legacy.ts`. **None were regenerated and none were dropped.**

- **29** map directly onto a new entry.
- **18** are unmapped, and 17 of those are *not* losses: they are per-āyah
  fragments of sequences now held as single entries — `ibrahim-2-128` and
  `ibrahim-2-129` are inside `2:127–129`; `musa-20-26/27/28` are inside
  `20:25–28`; `ibrahim-26-84…87` are inside `26:83–89`; `ibrahim-14-37/40/41`
  are inside `14:36–41`. Their Turkish needs *merging* into the combined
  entry's Turkish, which is editorial work, not recovery.
- The 18th, `nuh-23-26`, was a genuine gap and has been added as entry 5.
- The **5 ḥadīth entries** are held back pending grading (§5).

**23 new entries need Turkish written.** They are marked `new` in the table.

The English paraphrases are deliberately *not* migrated: all 47 were marked
`"translation_note": "Paraphrase"` with no translator, which cannot ship under
brief §2.3. Public-domain Pickthall and Yusuf Ali come through the same fetch
pipeline, fully attributed.

## 3. Theme taxonomy

16 themes, in `content/themes.ts`. Every one is phrased as a situation in the
second person rather than as an abstract noun — "When there seems no way out",
not "Hardship". Someone arriving mid-crisis is looking for their own situation
in a list, not looking up a subject index. The abstract term is demoted to the
description, where it still supports search.

No second level. A taxonomy that needs drilling has stopped being a door; where
an entry belongs in several places it carries several themes.

## 5. Open items — decisions needed before Phase 3

**Dāwūd has no duʿāʾ entry.** This is deliberate and follows the Phase 1
Appendix A check. 38:24 narrates that he sought forgiveness and prostrated but
does not quote his words, and 2:250 is the collective duʿāʾ of Ṭālūt's band
("those who crossed with him said") — it is filed under the companion section
as *The companions of Ṭālūt*, where Dāwūd was present but is not the speaker.
He therefore appears as a prophet with a page and no supplications of his own.
The honest options are to leave it as is, or to add 38:24 as biographical
context on his page without presenting it as a duʿāʾ. **I recommend the
latter** and have not implemented it pending your call.

**The 5 ḥadīth duʿāʾ are held back.** Bukhārī 834, 6369 and Muslim 843, 2720,
2721 all carry a collection and number but no grading, and the schema will not
validate a ḥadīth source without `grading` and `gradedBy`. These are all
well-known and almost certainly ṣaḥīḥ by their own collections' status, but
brief §2.1 requires each to be flagged for your manual review rather than
asserted by me. They need a pass before they re-enter.

**2:286 is not yet placed.** The closing duʿāʾ of al-Baqara is in the brief's
Appendix A under Muḥammad ﷺ, but the words are given to be said by the
believers rather than quoted as his own supplication. It belongs on the site.
Under "Muḥammad ﷺ" overstates it; the companion section is also not right.
A third grouping — *Duʿāʾ the Qurʾān teaches* — would hold it along with 3:8,
3:16, 3:147, 3:191–194 and 25:74. **This is a real expansion and I did not want
to invent a section without asking.**

**Transliteration is not yet generated.** The schema declares one scheme
site-wide; the legacy transliterations follow none consistently and collapse
every emphatic. They are preserved in the migration file but should be
regenerated wholesale rather than corrected. ALA-LC is the scholarly default
and is what I would pick; a simplified scheme is more readable for people who
have never seen the diacritics. Flagged for Phase 3.

**Context citations.** The 29 migrated `context` fields are carried at
`needs-review`. They are largely sound but none carries a tafsīr citation, and
brief §2.2 requires one before they ship. This is the largest remaining
editorial task and is not automatable.

## 6. What runs

```
npm run content:fetch      # resolve Arabic + translations, write lock file
npm run content:verify     # CI guard — fails on any drift
npm run content:inventory  # regenerate the table above
npm run typecheck          # tsc --noEmit, strict
npx tsx scripts/inspect-ayah.ts --compact 21:87   # choose excerpt boundaries
npx tsx scripts/migrate-legacy.ts                 # carry Turkish forward
```

`scripts/.cache/` holds fetched āyāt so repeated runs and CI do not hammer a
free public API. It is gitignored; `content.lock.json` is the source of truth.
